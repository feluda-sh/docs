The only agent that reads no source at all. It probes the deployed URL the way an outsider would.

surface scan 12 requests · 8s timeout
›GET / 200

Bounded by design

At most 12 passive requests, 8-second timeout, no redirects followed, and an SSRF guard that refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses before a socket is opened.

Continuous Protection

Detect, then block, then prove

1

Detect

Static rules, taint analysis and model reasoning identify the vulnerable pattern and its reachability.

→ finding with evidence
2

The in-app firewall stops injection, path traversal, SSRF and bot attacks at runtime — before the fix lands.

→ runtime enforcement
3

Prove

Every scan is a record. Every finding carries severity, CWE or CVE, evidence and a recommendation — ready for an auditor.

→ audit-ready trail

Evidence, not assertions

A finding is grounded twice: once before the model's cleanup pass and once after it, then a third time at the persistence layer. The path must appear in a real diff header or it does not get stored. Hallucinations fail closed.

Fails closed, loudly

A missing encryption key is an error, never a silent zero-findings result. A malformed job is acknowledged, not retried forever. A broken check becomes an explicit finding with its stack trace — it never takes its group down silently.
